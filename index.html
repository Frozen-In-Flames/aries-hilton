Aries (Drake) Hilton

Frozen In Flames

American music producer and software developer. Trap and hip-hop as Frozen In Flames. Open-source builder. Author.

Music

Hilton produces music as Frozen In Flames, working primarily in trap and hip-hop, with a second pillar in nu-metal, emo, grunge, and shoegaze guitar. His catalog runs 700+ songs deep, released independently through his label, Lucid Triangulation Records. He builds his own virtual instruments and keeps around 200 custom FX presets in rotation.

Software

Hilton publishes open-source software through his Frozen In Flames GitHub organization: a procedural VR/AR/MR/XR engine with zero asset files, a browser-based audio engine, a MIDI vocal training application, and patent-portfolio API software that turns any USPTO portfolio into per-patent REST APIs.

Technology

Hilton is a co-owner, chief technology officer, and co-founder of SunSiteVR LLC, a New Mexico-based spatial computing company working in augmented, virtual, and mixed reality. He is the inventor of the SSVR Aqua Drone, a multi-environment drone designed to operate on land, in the air, and underwater, combining lidar, sonar, and photogrammetry for surveying and inspection applications. SunSiteVR's drone program was featured as a case study in the New Mexico Small Business Assistance (NMSBA) Program's 2024 Perspectives report, published through Sandia National Laboratories.

Authorship

In 2026, Hilton self-published Energy Not Enemy, a 319-page book on building-scale thermal power systems: parabolic-profile Fresnel lens plus Tesla valve plus closed-loop boiler plus steam turbine, no photovoltaics. Published openly as a defensive publication. Free to read and buy or build from.
