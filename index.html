Aries (Drake) Hilton

Frozen In Flames

American music producer and software developer. Trap and hip-hop as Frozen In Flames. Open-source builder. Author.

Music

Hilton produces music as Frozen In Flames, working primarily in trap and hip-hop, with a second pillar in nu-metal, emo, grunge, and shoegaze guitar. His catalog runs 700+ songs deep, released independently through his label, Lucid Triangulation Records. He builds his own virtual instruments and keeps around 200 custom FX presets in rotation.

Software

Hilton publishes open-source software through his Frozen In Flames GitHub account: a procedural VR/AR/MR/XR engine with zero asset files, a browser-based audio engine, a MIDI vocal training application, and patent-portfolio API software that turns any USPTO portfolio into per-patent REST APIs.

Product Families

The work is organized in four product families. Each family is a set of open-source repositories under the Frozen In Flames GitHub account, connected by shared formats and shared data.

Music workstation. The production chain: VariTheory and the beatboxing-to-MIDI converter write Standard MIDI Files, Frozen In Flames Studio and the MIDI Vocal Trainer consume them, the Channel Strip and the Frozen In Flames plugin process the audio, fif-ears analyzes it, Kindling evolves a catalog over time, and Reactor renders the result as video. The Voice Asset Format carries a voice between engines the way a mesh format carries geometry.

Civic intelligence and Nation Kit. The public-record chain: Patent Portfolio APIs expose a USPTO portfolio as per-patent REST APIs, Mergers and Acquisitions for Peace screens candidates from that data, and the S&P 500 Corporate Stack maps the vendor landscape. The Nation Kit is the six settlement cores (the Ledger, the Registry, the Shield, the Sword, the Commons, and the Safety Net) that share one data directory and one settlement model. DeadRamp and DeadRamp Base apply the same method to the federal marketplace, rebuilding its offerings as open implementations.

Spatial twins. The three-dimensional chain: the Polyglot Procedural Engine is the runtime, with zero asset files and every visual generated in code; the digital twin on this page is its portrait surface; and the Energy Not Enemy working model is its walkable physical argument, a building-scale thermal power plant simulated in three independent implementations against the figures published in the book.

Host platform. The substrate: Frozen In Flames OS, a NixOS and FreeBSD host that runs the other families as local services, and Limit Breakers, the research programme that records each case where a platform limit was replaced by a locally built tool, with the evidence for each case.

Digital Twin

Hilton's bio carries a digital twin: a 3D likeness visitors can talk to. Ask it about any repository and it answers from the published record of that work, citing the repository each answer came from; if the record does not cover a question, it says so rather than improvising. Its voice is a model of his own, built the way his Voice Asset Format prescribes, and is in training; until it lands, spoken answers use an interim browser voice and are labeled as such.

Technology

Hilton is a co-owner, chief technology officer, and co-founder of SunSiteVR LLC, a New Mexico-based company built around the SSVR Aqua Drone. He is the inventor of the SSVR Aqua Drone, a multi-environment drone designed to operate on land, in the air, and underwater, combining lidar, sonar, and photogrammetry for surveying and inspection applications. The drone program was featured as a case study in the New Mexico Small Business Assistance (NMSBA) Program's 2024 Perspectives report, published through Sandia National Laboratories.

Authorship

In 2026, Hilton self-published Energy Not Enemy, a 319-page book on building-scale thermal power systems: parabolic-profile Fresnel lens plus Tesla valve plus closed-loop boiler plus steam turbine, no photovoltaics. Published openly as a defensive publication. Free to read and buy or build from.
