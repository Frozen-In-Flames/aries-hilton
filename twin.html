The twin is a three dimensional likeness of Aries Hilton, reconstructed from a 360 degree recorded orbit of a clay sculpt of him. The sculpt supplies the geometry. Colour was applied to that geometry at his direction: red hair and beard, blue eyes, purple shoulders, green torso. Colour moves no vertex. The likeness is a reconstruction, measured at 0.895 mean silhouette agreement with the recorded orbit, and is not claimed as exact.

Version history

The twin is rebuilt in the open. Each version below states what changed and what was measured.

  • v1, November 2026. First reconstruction of the head from the recorded orbit of the clay sculpt. Silhouette agreement with the orbit was the baseline every later version is measured against.
  • v2, November 2026. Refined carve. Mean silhouette agreement 0.886 across views; weakest single view 0.700.
  • v3, November 2026. Fine carve at 71,524 vertices, about 3.3 times the geometry density of v1, with brow, eye recess, and nose form restored. Mean silhouette agreement 0.895; weakest single view 0.835. Teeth, hair strands, beard curls, and the fade remain painted texture in the source recording, finer than the mesh the recording can support.
  • v4, November 2026. Colour applied to the v3 geometry at Aries's direction: red hair and beard, blue eyes. No vertex moved.
  • v5, November 2026. Current version. Presentation colours completed: purple shoulders, green torso, on the same v3 geometry. No vertex moved.
